https://github.com/qxoticai/qxotic/tree/main/jinfer[jinfer] ("JVM inference") is a pure-JVM inference engine from the https://qxotic.ai[Qxotic AI] project.
It runs GGUF models inside your application's JVM, without a separate inference server.

The integration is maintained by the Qxotic AI project, and its artifacts are published to Maven Central under the `com.qxotic` group.
